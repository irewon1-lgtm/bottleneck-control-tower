Tenova , a leading provider of sustainable solutions for the metals and mining industries, has been selected by Nippon Steel Corporation to supply a record-breaking Consteel ® Electric Arc Furnace (EAF) for Nippon Steel’s Yawata Area at Kyushu Works. Tenova has teamed up with GE Vernova , whose Direct Feed system will power the EAF.
