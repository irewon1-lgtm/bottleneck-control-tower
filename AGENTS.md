OTC excluded. No arbitrary market-cap cap. No return guarantees.

Separate facts, management plans, calculations, assumptions and UNKNOWN. Audit every base-case 2x; do not tune assumptions to a target or mechanically rank A above robust B. Funding and dilution are gates, not minor score penalties.

Final delivery freezes universe/decisions/assumptions/controls; first following regular-session open starts fixed 12 months. New opinions append; never reset old clock or remove losers. Use broad market, matched external universe with prespecified sampling, and original eligible BCT universe controls. No live trading, paid APIs, recurring jobs or public stock dashboard unless separately authorized.
