Engineered Hydrogen Compression Units Built for Performance, Precision, and Reliability
Total Hydrogen Solutions specializes in engineered hydrogen compression units, built on over 65 years of expertise. At the core of our systems are high-performance Haskel boosters, configured in single or multi-stage setups, tailored to meet exact pressure and application requirements. Each unit is fully integrated with precision components including control panels, gauges, and custom hydraulic power units, delivering safe and efficient compression for uses like 1,000 bar storage, fuel cell refueling, and electrolyzer support. Whether mobile, enclosed, or skid-mounted, our compression systems are ready to perform reliably, safely, and efficiently.
