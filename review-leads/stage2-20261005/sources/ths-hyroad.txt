Total Hydrogen Solutions Announces Commercial Fueling Agreement with Hyroad Energy to Support Hydrogen Powered Fleets in Texas
Posted by
Pneumatic and Hydraulic Company LLC
on
August 5, 2026
Katy, TX – August 3, 2026
– Total Hydrogen Solutions (THS), a division of Pneumatic and Hydraulic Company LLC, recently announced a commercial fueling agreement with
Hyroad Energy
to support the development and operation of a new hydrogen refueling station (HRS) in Katy, Texas. As part of the agreement, Hyroad has committed to the station’s full fueling capacity through a long-term offtake agreement, providing a reliable hydrogen supply solution for its growing fleet operations across Texas.
Designed, built, and operated by THS, the hydrogen refueling station features onsite hydrogen production through electrolysis, creating a localized fuel supply which reduces transportation costs and supply chain complexity. The facility is engineered to provide fast-fill fueling capabilities for heavy-duty hydrogen fuel cell trucks and trailers, supporting the increasing demand for zero-emission transportation solutions throughout the region.
The agreement marks a major milestone in the commercialization of hydrogen-powered transportation infrastructure and reflects nearly two years of collaboration between THS and Hyroad Energy. The station is expected to become fully operational later this year.
“We’re proud to partner with Hyroad Energy on this important step forward for hydrogen trucking in Texas. This station represents the kind of scalable, reliable infrastructure the industry needs, and it reflects our commitment to delivering end-to-end hydrogen solutions that help fleets transition with confidence,” said Mark Mire, Owner and CEO of Total Hydrogen Solutions.
The hydrogen refueling station in Katy, TX represents another step in THS’s strategy to develop scalable hydrogen infrastructure that connects hydrogen production directly to commercial transportation applications. Through turnkey project execution and lifecycle support, THS continues to help customers deploy practical hydrogen solutions that accelerate the transition to lower-emission energy systems.

About Hyroad Energy
Hyroad Energy delivers cutting-edge, zero-emission Class 8 hydrogen fuel cell trucks, designed to meet the needs of fleets while reducing their carbon footprint. Through its innovative pay-per-mile model, Hyroad eliminates the complexity and upfront costs traditionally associated with deploying zero-emission trucks. As a pioneer in hydrogen-powered transportation, Hyroad Energy is leading the charge toward a cleaner, more sustainable future. For more information, visit
