The Project
The geology was settled millions of years ago. The rest is settled now too.
Permits, infrastructure, feasibility, team. Hemerdon is ready.
A deposit that stands alone.
The Hemerdon Mine holds one of the most significant tungsten deposits on earth outside China. By reserve size, resource quality, and global ranking, this is a tier-one asset.
Learn more
A head start no new mine can buy.
Around US$300 million of infrastructure is already on site. The processing plant, crushing circuits, power supply, and access roads are all there. Hemerdon doesn't need to be built from scratch. It needs to be built properly. That's what we're doing.

A clear path to production
A revised feasibility study, all permits in place, and construction underway. This is a project in motion, with nameplate production targeted for H2 2027.

Understand the opportunity
01
The material behind everything that matters
Tungsten has the highest melting point of any known element, the tensile strength no other metal can match, and a list of critical applications that only gets longer.
Learn more
02
A supply problem hiding in plain sight
Over 80% of global tungsten supply has historically come from China. In 2025, China became a net importer. The concentration that made the market vulnerable is now making it critical.
Learn more
03
The window for Western producers
As China's export position shrinks, Western manufacturers, defence contractors, and governments are actively looking for reliable sovereign supply. The market is repricing. The timing for Hemerdon has never been better.

Sustainability
This isn't just about what comes out of the ground.
It's about what goes back into the community.
Real jobs, real investment
Over 300 direct roles spanning nine sectors, recruited locally wherever possible, with serious investment in training and clear pathways for progression. These are careers, not contracts.
Environmental accountability
Continuous monitoring of water, dust, noise, and groundwater. A fully funded £13.2 million restoration bond. 42,000 trees being planted across the site. We're held to a high standard, and we hold ourselves to a higher one.
A long-term neighbour
We've been part of the communities around Hemerdon since 2019, and we intend to stay that way. We maintain a presence at three parish councils, support local causes, and prioritise local suppliers.
